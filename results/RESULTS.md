# Results

The measured results have been withdrawn pending a validation check.

## Why

Every campaign run in BeamNG.tech was driven with the physics speed factor raised, so the simulation
ran faster than real time. The Dynamic ABS controller ticks from the physics step and is unaffected by
that. The game's own ABS may not be, and a manual run at normal speed produced a materially better
stock result on the same car and the same configuration: 0.948 g against the 0.885 g recorded in the
campaign, on a Gavril Roamer from 120 miles per hour.

If that difference is caused by the speed factor, then the stock baseline in those campaigns was
handicapped and every comparison against it overstates the controller. Publishing numbers that may be
flattering for that reason is not acceptable, so they are withdrawn until the cause is settled.

## What is being checked

Two blocks of stock stops on the same car at the same speed, one at normal speed and one at the raised
speed factor, back to back in a single session. If they differ, the affected campaigns will be rerun at
normal speed rather than corrected on paper.

The withdrawn data is kept in full offline. Nothing was deleted.

## Not affected

The Surface Suite, which covers ice, grass, sand, inclines, a jump and the bump sections, ran in
BeamNG.drive and never set a speed factor.
